08 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Know what runs.
Know who is responsible.

The practical questions to resolve before setup.

Does OrionQuant hold or manage my funds?

OrionQuant describes the engine as non-custodial software. Funds remain in your own broker account, with no provider access to deposits or withdrawals. You remain responsible for the broker relationship and trading risk.

Which platforms are supported?

The published product documentation lists MetaTrader 4/5 and cTrader. Compatibility with your specific broker, platform version and infrastructure must be checked before deployment. These are supported platforms, not a claim of partnership.

Where does the software run?

You operate the engine in your own environment, typically a third-party VPS or your own server. You are responsible for configuration, supervision and maintenance of that environment. A VPS can separate operation from your local device; it does not guarantee uninterrupted availability.

What is the testing phase for?

Technical evaluation checks installation, compatibility and rule execution in a non-production environment. Any trial availability, duration and cost must be confirmed in the license agreement. Testing does not make live trading risk-free.

What support is included, and can trading lose money?

Support concerns software, setup and platform integration within the agreed service scope, not investment advice. Trading involves market, operational and technology risks. There is no guarantee of returns or capital preservation; historical results and simulations do not predict future outcomes.
